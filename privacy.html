Privacy

Your details,
kept simple.

Sosick is a Nairobi business. This page explains what we collect when you order, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. Last updated 25 October 2026.

What we collect

  • What you ask us to get, and where to pick it up
  • Your name and phone number
  • Where to deliver: the area, any landmark you type, and your map location only if you tap Use my location
  • When you want it and how you'll pay (we never take card or M-Pesa PINs on this site)

Why

Only to deliver your order: to confirm the price with you, send a rider, and follow up if something goes wrong. We don't sell your details or use them for adverts.

Where it goes

  • WhatsApp: your order opens in WhatsApp and is sent to Sosick on 0112 275 881.
  • Our order list: a copy is saved in a secure database (Supabase, hosted in the EU) that only the Sosick team can sign in to.
  • Your rider: sees what they need to deliver: your name, phone, the item and where to bring it.

How long we keep it

Order records stay in our online system for up to 12 months so we can sort out returns and questions. After that they're removed from the online system and Sosick keeps one offline copy for the business's own records. You can still ask to see or delete your details in that copy.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask to see the details we hold about you, correct them, or have them deleted. Message us on WhatsApp at 0112 275 881 and we'll sort it out.

Verified Plugs

Sellers listed as Verified Plugs are independent businesses. When you visit their page, their own privacy rules apply.
